Open Engineering Bricks

Building engineering understanding, one brick at a time.

Welcome

Open Engineering Bricks explores how modular, LEGO-inspired building blocks can be used to represent, construct, visualize, and communicate complex engineering concepts.

A brick is more than a visual object. It is a reusable building block that can become part of a larger engineering model.

By combining physical-world metaphors with digital engineering, we make systems easier to understand, explore, and explain.

Our Mission

Our mission is to establish a reusable library of digital bricks that can be assembled into meaningful engineering representations.

We aim to make engineering:

* Visual — Complex concepts become recognizable objects.
* Composable — Simple building blocks form sophisticated structures.
* Accessible — Engineering becomes understandable beyond technical audiences.
* Reusable — Bricks can be shared across projects and applications.
* Automatable — Digital models can be generated through reproducible workflows.

What We Build

Open Engineering Bricks focuses on the foundational building blocks used throughout the Open Engineering ecosystem.

Brick Definitions

We use standardized brick specifications, including LDraw identifiers, to describe reusable geometric components.

Digital Brick Assets

Brick definitions can be transformed into digital assets suitable for visualization and interactive applications.

Brick Composition

Multiple bricks can be assembled into meaningful objects representing engineering concepts.

Brick Metadata

Bricks should carry sufficient metadata to identify their origin, geometry, dimensions, materials, and relationships.

Reusable Brick Libraries

Libraries provide a consistent source of reusable components for downstream engineering applications.

Ecosystem Integration

Open Engineering Bricks collaborates with other capabilities throughout the ecosystem.

Capability	Relationship
Open Engineering Models	Creates meaningful engineering models using bricks.
Open Engineering Rigs	Adds movement and behavior to compatible models.
Open Engineering Textures	Provides visual materials and surface details.
Open Engineering Architecture	Uses models to represent architectural elements.
Open Engineering Motion Pictures	Uses visual assets in animated productions.

Bricks should remain independent of the applications that consume them.

Engineering Principles

We follow five principles:

1. Open by design. Prefer documented and interoperable formats.
2. Define once, reuse everywhere. Avoid unnecessary duplication.
3. Separate geometry from meaning. Bricks describe building blocks; models describe engineering concepts.
4. Automate production. Favor repeatable transformations over manual asset creation.
5. Publish reusable artifacts. Make versioned outputs available to downstream consumers.
